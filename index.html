About Remember Us

Everyone has something they never said. Remember Us is a collection of letters written to people, memories, places, past versions of ourselves, or individuals we may never speak to again. You can sign your letter with a first name, initials, or remain anonymous.

This platform provides a quiet space for those unspoken words to exist, to be released into a public archive without the need for a recipient. It's a sanctuary for thoughts, feelings, and stories that were never meant to be sent, but deserved to be written.

Community Guidelines

  • Be respectful and mindful of others' experiences.
  • No hate speech, harassment, or personal attacks.
  • Signatures are optional; you may use a first name, initials, or 'Anonymous'. Please do not include full names or other personally identifiable information.
  • This is a space for introspection and release, not for public shaming or explicit content.

Privacy Information

All letters submitted to Remember Us are published with your chosen signature (first name, initials, or 'Anonymous'). We do not collect any personal data that could identify you. The content of your letter is public once submitted, so please ensure it does not contain any information you wish to keep private.
